Laptop Provision/Charges Policy 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. Objective  
The objective of this Policy is to support employees in maintaining the essential work-related devices 
required to perform their job responsibilities effectively. This provision ensures that employees have 
access to necessary technological equipment, specifically laptops, to effectiv ely perform their work-
related functions, either through a fixed allowance for personal laptops or through organization -
provided devices, as applicable. 
2. Scope and Eligibility 
This policy applies to all employees who are eligible to receive the Laptop Charg es or Laptop, based 
on their role, job requirements, and technical needs. Eligibility will be determined by the department 
head or immediate supervisor. 
This Policy governs: 
(a) provision of a fixed monthly disbursement (“Laptop Charges”) for use of personal l aptops 
(Bring Your Own Device / BYOD model); and 
(b) provision of organization -owned laptops for specific roles requiring advanced technical 
capabilities. 
Applicability of specific provisions shall be determined based on the category of device allocated to 
the employee.  
3. Laptop Charges 
“Laptop Charges” means a fixed monthly allowance paid to eligible employees to support costs 
associated with procurement, maintenance, and use of a personal laptop for official purposes. 
4. Disbursement of Laptop Charges 
(a) Monthly Laptop Charges: Eligible employees will receive a fixed monthly Laptop Charges of 
INR 5,000/- (Indian Rupees Five Thousand Only) . This is intended to support employees in 
covering the costs associated with the purchase, maintenance, and periodic upgrade of 
personal laptops necessary for performing work duties. 
(b) Payment Schedule: The Laptop Charges shall be disbursed on a monthly basis, within the first 
week of each month. The payment shall be subject to applicable statutory deductions, 
including income tax, as per prevailing laws. 
(c) Non-refundable: The Laptop Charges are a fixed monthly payment and are not reimbursable 
or adjustable against any actual costs, purchases, or expenses incurred by the employee. Any

(d) Duration of Benefit: The Laptop Charges shall be payable for a period of 24 months from the 
employee’s date of joining or until the employee’s last working day, whichever is earlier. 
(e) Post-Completion Use: Upon completion of the 24-month period, the employee shall continue 
to use and maintain the same laptop for official purposes, provided that the devi ce remains 
functional and meets the technical and security requirements prescribed by the organization. 
Cessation of Laptop Charges shall not entitle the employee to claim any additional allowance, 
reimbursement, replacement, or upgrade, unless otherwise a pproved by the competent 
authority. 
(f) Data Handover and Removal upon Separation:  Upon resignation, termination of 
employment, or any other separation from the organization, employees must transfer, return, 
or otherwise provide access to all official data, fi les, records, and work -related information 
stored on their personal devices to the organization. Employees must also permanently 
remove any company data from their personal devices upon confirmation from the IT 
Department or their reporting manager and comply with all applicable data security and exit 
clearance requirements. 
(g) Suspension or Discontinuation 
Laptop Charges may be suspended or discontinued in the following circumstances: 
i. change in role or eligibility;  
ii. provision of an organization-owned laptop; 
iii. extended leave, absence, or suspension (as per applicable HR policies); or 
iv. non-compliance with this Policy or IT guidelines. 
 
5. Organization-Provided Laptops (High-End Laptops) 
For employees whose roles require high-performance laptops (e.g., Information Technology, Software 
Development, Data Analysis, Video Editing, Graphic Design), the organization will provide these 
laptops directly as per business and role requirements. The distinction between standard and high -
end laptops will be made by the department head, based on the specific needs of the employee’s job.  
(a) Eligibility for High-End Laptops: Employees whose roles require advanced processing power, 
higher memory capacity, or specialized hardware/software requirements shall be eligible for 
a high-end laptop. Such devices will be procured and provided directly by the organization. 
(b) Approval Process: The department head will assess the employee’s role, responsibilities, and 
technical requirements to determine the necessity of a high -performance laptop. Final 
approval shall be subject to organizational guidelines and budgetary considerations. 
(c) Ownership, Care, and Maintenance:  All high -end laptops provided under this policy shall 
remain the property of the organization. The organization shall bear the cost of procurement, 
maintenance and repairs, unless damage is caused due to negligence or misuse  of the 
employees. Employees are responsible for the proper care and safe usage of the device.

(d) Incident Reporting:  In the event of malfunction, loss, damage, or technica l issues, the 
employee must promptly notify the IT Department for necessary support and resolution. 
(e) Use and Return:  Employees shall use organization -provided laptops primarily for official 
purposes and shall return the device in good condition upon separation or when required. 
6. Technical and Security Requirements for Personal Laptops – Annexure-1 
Employees using personal laptops under this Policy must ensure compliance with the requirements 
specified in Annexure-1.  
The employee's personal laptop will be subject to review, verification and compliance checks by the 
IT/Admin Department or any authorized personnel. Failure to meet or maintain the prescribed 
requirements will result in necessary action. 
7. Employee Responsibilities 
(a) Device Maintenance and Security  of Personal Laptops : Employees are responsible for the 
upkeep, maintenance, and repair of their personal laptops. 
(b) Work-Related Use of the organization-provided Laptop: The primary purpose of the laptop 
given by the organization is for performing work-related tasks. While limited personal use is 
permitted, it should not interfere with work performance or violate company policies. 
(c) Confidentiality and Data Protection:  Employees must ensure that their devices safeguard 
sensitive company information, using secu re storage practices, and reporting any security 
incidents such as device theft or unauthorized access. 
(d) Security Compliance: Every Employees shall comply with all IT policies, including installation 
of security tools, system updates, and access controls as required by the organization. 
(e) Incident Reporting: Employees must promptly report any loss, theft, malfunction, or security 
breach involving their device. 
8. Limitation of Liability 
The organization shall not be liable for: 
(a) loss, theft, or damage to personal laptops; 
(b) any personal data stored on employee-owned devices; or 
(c) any costs incurred beyond the prescribed Laptop Charges. 
 
9. Termination of Benefits 
Eligibility for Laptop Charges shall cease upon termination of employment. Any dues shall be settled 
in accordance with final settlement processes. 
10. Exception/ Modification 
Any exception to this policy would require the prior approval of the CEO of MyGov. The policy may be 
revised/ modified from time to time.

4. Data Protection 
 Sensitive data should be encrypted 
 Regular backups to approved storage 
 The use of USB storage devices shall be restricted. Where their use is authorized, 
the devices must be password-protected and encrypted 
 Do not copy official data to personal cloud storage or personal email or USB 
 
5. Network Security 
(a) Mandatory Guidelines for Using the NIC Network: 
i. Installation of any software on organizational systems shall be undertaken only with 
prior approval and authorization from the IT Support Team. 
ii. All users, including permanent and contractual personnel working within NIC 
premises, shall register their Wi -Fi-enabled devices through the eForms  portal 
(eforms.nic.in). Official work shall be conducted exclusively through the authorized 
and registered Wi-Fi SSIDs, namely ANANT, SARAS, or VAYU. 
iii. All newly deployed systems (Windows, Linux, or macOS) and any systems connected 
to the NIC network that do not have endpoint protection shall be equipped with the 
antivirus/endpoint security solutions (UEM/EDR) prescribed by NIC. 
iv. Only genuine and duly licensed software and operating systems shall be used on 
organizational devices. The installation or use of pirated, unauthorized, or unlicensed 
software is strictly prohibited. 
v. Certain network ports may be blocked by the SentinelOne endpoint security solution, 
potentially impacting applications that utilize such ports. In such cases, the concerned 
application owner shall coordinate with the IT Team to obtain the necessary port and 
IP address whitelisting after due review and approval. 
 
(b) Prohibited Practices on the NIC Network 
i. Users shall not connect or operate any unmanaged network device within the 
organization's network infrastructure i.e., wireless access points, swit ch, routers etc. in 
NIC network. 
ii. Users shall avoid the installation and use of potentially unwanted software (PUS) on 
organizational systems like Anydesk, Teamviewer etc. 
iii. Do not click on unknown or suspicious links received via email. 
iv. Do not make personal Wi-Fi hotspot from your devices in LAN to get internet connectivity 
on other devices. 
v. Do not allow personal smart devices (e.g., smartwatches, fitness trackers)  to connect to 
the MeitY (NIC) network unless on a dedicated guest network with restricted access. 
vi. Employee shall not save passwords in browsers or use default passwords on devices and 
systems. 
vii. Do not disable security features  (e.g., firewalls, encryption, MFA) without documented  
risk assessments and approvals.
